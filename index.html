How it works

Everything for posting the apartment is here: the photos, the post, and a link to each group. Work through the steps below in order.

  • Works best with the Facebook app installed and logged in on this phone.
  • Your progress saves automatically, so you can stop and come back anytime.
  • Stuck? Take a screenshot and text it to Tyler.

💬Answering messages

When someone messages you, find their question below, tap Copy, then paste it into your reply. Change anything in [brackets].

🚩 If someone asks you to pay anything, send a code, or says they can't see it in person and will "send a deposit," don't reply. That's a scam.

🆘 If something goes wrong

Facebook asked me to log in

Log in with your normal Facebook email and password. You only need to do this once.

I tapped Paste but nothing showed up

Come back to this page and tap Open this group again. That copies the post again. Then go back to Facebook and try Paste again.

My phone asked "Allow Paste?"

Tap Allow Paste. That's normal.

I can't find "Write something…"

You probably need to join the group first. Look for a blue Join group button near the top and tap it. If the group has to approve you, come back here and tap Skip this group for now.

The group asked me questions to join

Answer simply and honestly, for example: "I'm looking for someone to take over my apartment lease in Weehawken." Tap Submit, then come back here and tap Skip this group for now. Try that group again tomorrow.

After posting it says "Pending" or "Waiting for approval"

That's normal. Some groups check posts first. Come back here and tap the green button anyway.

I closed everything and lost my place

Open this link again. It remembers where you were.

Something else

Take a screenshot (press the side button and the volume-up button at the same time) and text it to Tyler.

Good to know

  • Don't put a phone number or email in the post. Let people message you on Facebook.
  • Scam warning: never take or send money before someone tours the apartment, and never share a code that's texted to you.
  • Tours: weekdays after 4:30 PM, weekends 8 AM–6 PM.
