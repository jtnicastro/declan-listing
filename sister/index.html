1Before you start

  1. Make sure your Facebook account has a profile photo. Some groups automatically reject posts from accounts without one.
  2. When a group asks questions to join, just be honest: "I'm helping my brother find someone to take over his apartment lease in Weehawken."
  3. Don't post in groups where Tyler or his co-tenant already posted (list at the bottom). Two copies of the same apartment looks like a scam.

2Save the photos

15 full-size photos. The living room photo should be first in every post.

3Join and post in these groups

These are the groups where listings and budgets are closest to Tyler's price (most local NJ groups are mostly rooms under $2,000, so we skipped them). Best time to post: today around 12–1 PM or tonight around 8–9 PM.

How to post (on your phone)

  1. Tap Copy post for that group (above).
  2. Tap Open group, then Join (answer the questions if it asks). Some groups take a few hours to approve you.
  3. Once you're in, tap Write something…, press and hold, and tap Paste.
  4. Tap the green Photo button, pick the living room photo first, then all the others.
  5. Tap Post, then tick Posted here.

4Comment on Tyler's live posts

A new comment pushes a post back up in the group. One comment per post at each time below is plenty. Never just write "bump", and don't pretend to be someone interested.

5When someone messages you

Reply quickly and connect them with Tyler (send him their name, or share his Facebook profile with them). Don't share the apartment number or anyone's phone number in the group.
